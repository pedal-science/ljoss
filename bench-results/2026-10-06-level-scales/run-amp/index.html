What the levels mean

A measured map from the signal at the pedal's input jack to every number the app can show: the engine's input, node and output levels, the interface's own readings of the same signal, and the loudness a listener would hear. Measured by playing known signals out of an SSL 2+ Mk II — one output looped straight back as an analogue reference, one into the pedal — while polling engine.levels at 20 Hz and recording the pedal's own converters.
